<objective>
Produce the manual on-hardware verification record proving the three physical resilience
scenarios pass against REAL WinRT (which the 03-01/03-02 mocks cannot prove). This is the
hardware half of D-06's test-split, mirroring Phase 2's `02-WINDOWS-VERIFICATION.md`:

- **SC#1 — sleep/wake:** sleep the Windows PC, wake it; confirm the daemon reconnects and
  pushes a FRESH usage update within 2 poll cycles (120s). Capture observed latency.
- **SC#2 — out-of-range:** carry the Clawdmeter out of BLE range and back; confirm
  auto-reconnect with no user action.
- **SC#3 — power-cycle:** power the Clawdmeter off and on; confirm pickup on the next scan.
- **SC#4 — no restart:** confirm the SAME daemon process recovered from all three without a
  restart (single continuous run, one PID).

Because this requires physical hardware and human observation, the run is a
`checkpoint:human-verify` task. The daemon is launched and the scenarios driven by the
operator; the executor captures the console log, fills the record, and the operator attests
PASS/FAIL with timings.

Purpose: Closes BLE-03 / ROADMAP SC#1–4 against real WinRT — the proof mocks cannot give.
Depends on 03-01 and 03-02 so the hardened code is what gets exercised.
Output: `.planning/phases/03-resilience/03-WINDOWS-VERIFICATION.md`.
</objective>
